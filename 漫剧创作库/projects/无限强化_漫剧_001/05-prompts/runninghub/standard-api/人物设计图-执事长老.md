## 1. 设计图规格

### 整体布局
- **排版**：三等分横向排版（3 行 × 3 列）
- **背景**：纯灰色背景（#808080）
- **画面要求**：不得出现文字和边框

### 第一层：人物三视图
| 位置 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| 左 | 正面全身（老者挺立，手持权杖，严肃庄重） |
| 中 | 侧面全身（清瘦但笔直的老者身形） |
| 右 | 背面全身（白发束冠，灰色长袍后摆，权杖细节） |

### 第二层：面部特写 × 3 表情
| 位置 | 表情 | 细节描述 |
|------|------|----------|
| 左 | 严肃板正 | 白眉微蹙，长须垂落，面无表情不苟言笑 |
| 中 | 宣布仪式 | 微抬下巴，嘴唇张开正在说话，权威感 |
| 右 | 震惊意外 | 眉毛高高挑起，眼睛睁大，难以置信 |

### 第三层：人物动作 × 3
| 位置 | 动作 | 细节描述 |
|------|------|----------|
| 左 | 高台主持 | 站在高台上，手持权杖，另一手平举示意开始 |
| 中 | 点评审视 | 一手抚须，一手背于身后，微微点头或不屑撇嘴 |
| 右 | 后退震惊 | 身体后退半步，权杖几乎脱手，满脸错愕 |

## 2. 核心提示词 (Prompt)

### 角色描述 (Character)
Chinese comic character design sheet of Elder Steward (the ceremony officiant, testing proctor), three-row layout on solid gray background.
Row 1 - full body three-view:
left: front view elderly man standing tall and straight holding wooden ceremonial staff serious dignified bearing not stooped despite age,
center: side view slender but upright elderly physique thin but wiry martial artist foundation visible in posture,
right: back view white hair in topknot with simple crown grey robe trailing hem wooden staff details.
Row 2 - facial close-ups with distinct expressions:
left: stern serious white eyebrows slightly furrowed long white beard flowing down face completely expressionless unsmiling officious demeanor,
middle: announcing ceremony chin lifted slightly mouth open mid-speech projecting authority ritual voice,
right: shocked surprised eyebrows raised extremely high eyes widened disbelief something unexpected occurred during testing.
Row 3 - action poses:
left: presiding on elevated platform holding staff in one hand other hand gesturing flat to signal ceremony beginning formal official posture,
center: evaluating assessment one hand stroking beard thoughtfully other hand behind back slight nod or dismissive lip curl judging participants,
right: recoiling shocked body stepping back half-step staff nearly slipping from grasp face full of astonishment dismay.

### 特殊标记 (Special Marks)
Authoritative aura, uncompromising gaze, physically grounded stance despite his age.

### 场景与氛围 (Environment)
Grey and desolate post-apocalyptic ancient Chinese background, massive negative space emphasizing solemnity, oppressive atmosphere, high contrast shadows.

### 风格锚点 (Style Anchor - 强制追加)
