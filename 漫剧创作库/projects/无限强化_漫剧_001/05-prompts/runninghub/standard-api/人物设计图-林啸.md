## 1. 设计图规格

### 整体布局
- **排版**：三等分横向排版（3 行 × 3 列）
- **背景**：纯灰色背景（#808080）
- **画面要求**：不得出现文字和边框

### 第一层：人物三视图
| 位置 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| 左 | 正面全身（端肃站姿，双手背于身后，威严） |
| 中 | 侧面全身（展示家主袍服轮廓和身材匀称） |
| 右 | 背面全身（家主令牌垂于腰后，深色长袍背影） |

### 第二层：面部特写 × 3 表情
| 位置 | 表情 | 细节描述 |
|------|------|----------|
| 左 | 冷漠审视 | 面无表情，眼神居高临下审视，嘴唇紧闭下压 |
| 中 | 阴鸷算计 | 眼神微眯成缝，嘴角隐约上扬，城府极深 |
| 右 | 权力威压 | 眉头紧锁目光如刀，不怒自威的压迫感 |

### 第三层：人物动作 × 3
| 位置 | 动作 | 细节描述 |
|------|------|----------|
| 左 | 端坐高位 | 坐在家主位上，双手搭扶手，俯视下方 |
| 中 | 背手审视 | 双手背后转身回头，锐利目光扫视 |
| 右 | 下达命令 | 一手指向前方/下方，命令姿态，不容置疑 |

## 2. 角色特征锚点（必须保持一致）

- 年龄：40-45 岁中年男子
- 体型：中等偏高，身材匀称，**气质威严不怒自威**
- 发型：深色长发束于脑后整齐利落，**鬓角少许银丝**（权力与年龄暗示）
- 面部：阴鸷面容颧骨略高，眼窝深邃眼神透着算计，**眉间竖纹**，嘴角微微下压
- 肤色：偏白（常年室内掌权）
- 服装：**深色（近黑）家主长袍**——暗金纹饰绣家族徽记，金色腰带，肩部暗金纹饰，整体深色系+暗金点缀
- 配饰：腰间悬挂家主令牌

## 2. 核心提示词 (Prompt)

### 角色描述 (Character)
Chinese comic character design sheet of Lin Xiao (the patriarch, mastermind villain), three-row layout on solid gray background.
Row 1 - full body three-view:
left: front view stern authoritative standing posture hands clasped behind back chin slightly raised imposing presence,
center: side view showing well-proportioned middle-aged build and patriarch robe silhouette dignified bearing,
right: back view clan leader token hanging at waist back of dark robe family embroidery visible.
Row 2 - facial close-ups with distinct expressions:
left: cold indifferent inspection expressionless face looking down from above tight-pressed lips corners turned slightly downward,
middle: calculating sinister squinted eyes narrowed to slits corners of mouth subtly raised deep scheming intelligence visible between brows,
right: oppressive authority furrowed brows tight gaze like a blade natural commanding presence intimidating without anger.
Row 3 - action poses:
left: seated on high patriarch throne hands resting on armrests looking down at subordinates absolute authority position,
center: hands behind back turning head to glance back sharp assessing scan of surroundings,
right: pointing command gesture one finger directed downward or forward unquestionable order tone in body language.

### 特殊标记 (Special Marks)
Middle-aged man 40-45yo, dark near-black clan leader patriarch long robe with subtle gold embroidery, distinctive silver streaks at temples.

### 场景与氛围 (Environment)
Grey and desolate post-apocalyptic ancient Chinese background, massive negative space emphasizing isolation, desaturated color palette, oppressive atmosphere, high contrast shadows, cinematic composition.

### 风格锚点 (Style Anchor - 强制追加)
Style anchor — bold thick black outlines, Japanese manga style fused with Chinese manhua aesthetic, cel-shaded flat colors with hard edge shadows, graphic novel illustration quality, grim atmosphere, professional comic book illustration, cel-shaded with subtle gradients, hyper-cool character design, strictly obeying physical lighting rules, no text no border 8k quality.
